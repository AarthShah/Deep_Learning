DKTE Society's Textile and Engineering Institute (DKTE) is an autonomous engineering college located in Ichalkaranji, Maharashtra, India.

Overview:
- Full Name: DKTE Society's Textile and Engineering Institute
- Location: Ichalkaranji, Kolhapur, Maharashtra, India
- Established: 1982
- Affiliation: Shivaji University
- Autonomous Status: Yes
- Accreditation: NBA, NAAC Accredited

Courses Offered:
- B.Tech (UG Programs):
  - Computer Science & Engineering
  - Mechanical Engineering
  - Textile Engineering
  - Electronics Engineering
  - Electrical Engineering
  - Civil Engineering
  - Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

- M.Tech (PG Programs):
  - Textile Engineering
  - Mechanical Engineering
  - Electronics Engineering

- Diploma Courses:
  - Textile Manufacturing
  - Mechanical
  - Electrical
